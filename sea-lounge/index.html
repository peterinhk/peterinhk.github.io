    From the Log

    The Sea Lounge

    The Sea Lounge sits on North Sound, Grand Cayman — half watersports outfit, half bar & grill, built for the stretch of afternoon between a snorkel trip and a sunset cruise. We run the flats by day and keep the kitchen open for whoever's on the dock, tourist or local.

    Two sailings, a small stack of snorkel gear, and a lunch menu built for pickup — that's the whole operation, kept simple on purpose.
